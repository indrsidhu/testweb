Health guide

Practical articles from the same specialties you will find in the hospital.

2026-07-12

Joint Pain, Sports Injury or Arthritis? When to Visit an Orthopaedic Doctor

Persistent joint pain, swelling after injury, or stiffness that limits walking deserves a specialist review. Early assessment can separate simple strain from ligament injury or arthritis that needs structured treatment.

2026-07-05

How to Choose Physiotherapy for Back and Joint Pain

Look for a programme tied to diagnosis, not only machines. Good physiotherapy explains the plan, measures progress, and coordinates with your orthopaedic or medical doctor.

2026-06-28

10 Signs You May Need Knee Replacement Surgery

Night pain, giving way, failed medicines and physiotherapy, and X-rays showing advanced arthritis are common reasons patients discuss replacement with a knee surgeon.

2026-06-20

Choosing a Gynaecology Hospital for Pregnancy and Delivery

Maternity care should include antenatal visits, a plan for high-risk pregnancy, paediatric cover at birth, and a team you can reach at odd hours.

2026-06-14

General Dentist or Specialist in Chandigarh?

Routine check-ups and fillings stay with a family dentist. Implants, braces, oral surgery, and complex paediatric cases often need a specialist under the same hospital roof.

2026-06-08

Vaccination Schedule Explained by Child Specialists

Keeping immunisations on time protects infants and the wider family. Our paediatric team walks parents through the national schedule and catch-up doses.

2026-05-30

Fertility Myths Busted by Gynaecologists

Age, irregular cycles, and untreated infections matter more than most internet advice. A structured evaluation is kinder than waiting another year on a guess.

2026-05-22

What Safe Pregnancy Care Looks Like

Regular scans, blood pressure checks, anaemia treatment, and a documented birth plan reduce surprises. High-risk mothers need a hospital with ICU and neonatal backup.

2026-05-15

ENT Care for Sinus, Allergy and Hearing Problems

Blocked nose that lasts months, repeated ear infections, or hearing drop should not be ignored. Endoscopic and audiology pathways exist for precise treatment.

2026-05-08

Expert Diabetes Care with Endocrinology Support

Good diabetes care is more than a single sugar reading. Medication, diet, kidney and eye screening, and thyroid review belong in one follow-up plan.

2026-04-28

When Stress and Autoimmune Symptoms Need a Rheumatologist

Unexplained joint swelling, rashes, and fatigue can be inflammatory disease — not “just stress”. Internal medicine and rheumatology can sort the cause.

2026-04-18

How to Know You Need a Psychiatrist

If sleep, work, or relationships are breaking down, a confidential psychiatric review is appropriate. Asking for help is clinical care, not a last resort.

2026-04-10

How to Know When You Need a Heart Specialist

Chest discomfort, breathlessness on mild effort, or a strong family history of heart disease should trigger an ECG and cardiology opinion.

2026-04-02

Expert Care for Migraine and Stroke

Sudden weakness, speech change, or the worst headache of your life is an emergency. Recurrent migraine also deserves a neurologist, not only painkillers.

2026-03-25

When to Visit a Dermatologist for Skin and Hair Problems

Acne that scars, sudden hair fall, or rashes that do not settle with home care are reasons to see a skin specialist.

2026-03-18

Who Is the Right Orthopaedic Doctor for Knee and Joint Pain?

Knee and joint pain that lasts beyond rest deserves a named orthopaedic review. Look for joint-replacement and sports-injury experience, not only a nearby clinic.

2026-03-12

Common Dental Problems and Where to Get Treatment

Tooth pain, bleeding gums, and broken teeth should be seen promptly. CHD City Hospital dentistry covers check-ups, root canal, implants, and paediatric visits.

2026-03-05

Online vs In-Clinic Psychiatric Consultation

Video follow-up can help with medication reviews. First assessments, child psychiatry, and crisis care are usually better in person at the hospital.

2026-02-26

Cardiology Care for Heart Symptoms in Chandigarh

A hospital with ECG, medical cardiology, and ICU backup is the safer place for chest pain than a standalone clinic.

2026-02-18

Choosing a Hospital for Advanced Medical Care

Advanced care means ICU, HDU, a modular OT, and specialists who talk to each other. That is the model at this 25-bed NABH hospital.
